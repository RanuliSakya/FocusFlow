Features

Everything you need to do your best work

Built around how people actually think and work — not how project managers want them to.

Energy-aware

Check in with your energy level each morning — high, medium, or low — and FocusFlow surfaces tasks that match how you feel right now.

Focus contexts

Organise tasks by Deep Work, Quick Win, Collaborative, or Admin — so you instantly know what each task demands from you cognitively.

Built-in timer

Enter distraction-free Focus Mode with a countdown timer for each task. Start, pause, and reset without leaving the page.

Subtask tracking

Break tasks into subtasks and tick them off as you go — even during a focus session, so your progress is always visible.

Fully responsive

Works seamlessly across desktop, tablet, and mobile so you can check and update your tasks wherever you are.

Private by default

Your data stays in your browser. No servers, no syncing, no surveillance — just you and your tasks.
